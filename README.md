 🔐 QuantumSeal AI
 AI-Powered Product Authentication & Counterfeit Detection
 🚀 About the Project

QuantumSeal AI is an intelligent product authentication platform designed to identify counterfeit products and verify product authenticity.

The platform allows users to scan a product's QR code or enter a verification link provided on the product packaging. It then checks the product information against manufacturer-provided databases and uses quantum-based verification technology to help determine whether the product is genuine or potentially counterfeit.

Our goal is to build a smarter, more secure and reliable product verification ecosystem.

 ✨ Key Features

- **QR Code Scanning:** Scan product QR codes for instant verification.
- **Link-Based Verification:** Verify products using manufacturer-provided URLs.
- **Manufacturer Database Integration:** Cross-check product details against manufacturer records.
- **Product Authentication:** Identify potential counterfeit or suspicious products.
- **Quantum-Based Verification:** Incorporate quantum-based verification techniques into the product validation process.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Simple and accessible product verification experience.
 ⚙️ How It Works

1. Open the QuantumSeal AI platform.
2. Scan the QR code on the product package or provide its verification link.
3. The system extracts the available product information.
4. Product details are checked against manufacturer-provided databases.
5. The verification mechanism evaluates the product information.
6. The platform presents the verification result to the user.
